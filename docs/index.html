🪪 Learn AWS the school way — IAM & EC2

The foundations course of the school series: the two AWS ideas every other course stands on. IAM is the ID-card office — who may do what. EC2 is the rented computer — the desk everything ultimately runs on. ECR logins, EKS nodes, CI roles: it's all these two, wearing different costumes.
